# Customer Churn & Retention Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the most important challenges for subscription-based and service-based businesses. Losing customers directly affects revenue, profitability, and long-term growth. This project focuses on analyzing customer churn and retention patterns using **Power BI** and **DAX** to identify why customers leave, which customer groups are most at risk, and what actions can help improve retention.

The goal of this project is to build an interactive business intelligence dashboard that provides insights into customer behavior, churn drivers, customer lifetime value, and retention opportunities.

This project is currently in progress and will be developed step by step, including data cleaning, data modeling, DAX measure creation, dashboard design, and business recommendations.

## Project Title

**Customer Churn & Retention Analytics Dashboard**

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall customer churn and retention performance
- Identify customer segments with high churn risk
- Understand the key factors influencing customer churn
- Estimate Customer Lifetime Value
- Analyze churn behavior across tenure-based cohorts
- Segment customers based on value, risk, and loyalty
- Recommend data-driven retention strategies

---

## Business Questions

This project is designed to answer the following business questions:

### 1. Executive Overview: Overall Customer Health

- What is the overall churn rate, retention rate, and total number of active vs churned customers?
- How much estimated monthly revenue or customer value is being lost due to churn?
- Which high-level customer category contributes most to churn and revenue loss?

### 2. Churn Profile: Who Is Leaving?

- Which customer segments have the highest churn rate based on contract type, tenure, payment method, or service type?
- Are new customers more likely to churn compared with long-tenure customers?
- Do high monthly charge customers churn more than low or medium monthly charge customers?

### 3. Churn Drivers: Why Are Customers Leaving?

- What are the strongest factors influencing customer churn according to Key Influencers analysis?
- Which combination of customer attributes creates the highest churn risk?
- Does pricing, contract flexibility, or payment method appear to be the biggest churn driver?

### 4. Retention Analysis: Who Is Staying?

- Which customer groups have the highest retention rate?
- What characteristics are common among loyal, long-tenure customers?
- Which contract types, services, or payment methods are associated with stronger retention?

### 5. Customer Value & CLV: Financial Impact

- What is the total estimated Customer Lifetime Value and average CLV per customer?
- How much CLV is lost from churned customers?
- Which high-value customer groups are most at risk of churning?

### 6. Cohort / Tenure Analysis: When Do Customers Leave?

- At which tenure stage is churn the highest?
- How does churn rate change as customer tenure increases?
- Which tenure group contributes the most to lost revenue or lost CLV?

### 7. Risk Segmentation: Who Needs Attention First?

- How many customers fall into high-risk, medium-risk, and low-risk churn categories?
- Which customers are both high-value and high-risk?
- Which risk segment should be prioritized first for retention campaigns?

### 8. Customer Segmentation: What Types of Customers Exist?

- What are the main customer segments based on churn status, tenure, and customer value?
- Which customer segment has the highest churn rate and revenue loss?
- Which segment should the business protect, grow, or re-engage?

### 9. Actionable Recommendations

- Which customer group should the business target first to reduce churn?
- What retention actions can reduce churn among high-risk customers?
- Which business strategy would protect the most customer value: contract incentives, pricing changes, payment method changes, or service improvements?

## Expected Insights

By completing this project, the dashboard should help identify:

- Which customer groups are most likely to churn
- Whether churn is higher among new or long-term customers
- Which contract types and payment methods are linked with churn
- Whether high-value customers are being lost
- At what stage customers are most likely to leave
- Which customers should be prioritized for retention campaigns
- What business actions can reduce churn and protect revenue
